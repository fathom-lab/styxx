## 3. What the receipt says beyond the counts

**Which guard saw what — the separation held.** Of the 96 mutants that MUTE-1 let through and
MUTE-2 killed: 27 were killed by the manifest test and nothing else; 32 by a behavioural case and
nothing else; 37 by both. The 37 are M-STEP and M-JOB mutants where the manifest went red *and*
the deleted step's behavioural case vanished — the vanishing the preregistration said would be
there and said not to read as detection. Remove it from the count and the behavioural guard's
own kills are the 33 swallows: exactly the shape it was written for, and none other.

**32 of the 33 killed swallows were killed by exactly one test — the behavioural case for that
step.** The one exception, MUTE-016 (the swallow of `Discover changed submissions`), was also
killed by the guard from #137 that runs that step's shell, as in MUTE-1. Every other swallow in
the repository had, until this run, no test that could see it.

**The manifest-only kills are the checks nothing else in the suite knows exist.** The 27: all
eight surviving triggers of MUTE-1 (`audit-claims`, `diffgate`, `gauntlet-pr`, `nightly-heavy`,
`publish`, `replications`, `telescope`, `test`); all eight `if:` guards; the `publish` and
`github-release` jobs and the `diffgate` job, which have no `run:` steps to hold behaviourally;
the three npm scripts (`build`, `test`, `test:watch`); and the deletion of the five exempt steps.
For all of these the only thing the suite can now say is *it used to be declared and is not* —
which is more than it could say before, and is all a manifest can say.

**The five survivors, read (a judgement, marked as one).** MUTE-018, 024, 031, 050, 073 are the
swallows of the five steps that reach no external command when every `${{ … }}` is `x`:
two gauntlet steps that loop over an empty submission list or only print, the leaderboard
dependency install that is conditional on a requirements file, nightly-heavy's step summary, and
the telescope key check, which sets outputs from environment variables and calls nothing. In this
harness a `|| true` on any of them changes nothing a test can observe. Three of the five are
gated (`if:`) steps whose *guards* are now pinned by the manifest, so the route "make this step
never run" is closed even though the route "make this step never fail" is not. Whether any of
the five *should* be able to fail is a question about the workflow, not about the guard, and is
left where it belongs.

## 5. What this does not say

- **Loudness is not truth.** 114 of 120 cuts are now visible. Zero of 120 checks have been shown
  correct. The manifest says a check is still declared; the behavioural guard says a step can
  still go red. Neither says a step does the right thing when its tools succeed, and no
  mutation of the harness could.
- **The kill rate is now easy to inflate and should not be reported alone.** A manifest that
  pinned step text would kill everything and mean nothing; this one does not, and the receipt's
  per-mutant `killed_by` is what shows that (P2, P4). Any future MUTE run on this repository
  should be read by *which test* killed a mutant, not by how many were killed.
- **The five survivors are equivalent-ish mutants in this harness**, not five open defects, and
  are named so that nobody spends an afternoon on them.
- **The oracle is the suite, not CI**, as in MUTE-1. A deleted `uses: actions/checkout` is still
  out of scope.
- **The instrument's v1 rule counts a vanished test as a kill.** This run relied on the
  preregistration to say in advance which kills would be vanishings (P4) so the count could be
  read. The instrument should record the split itself; that is a one-field change to the receipt
  and is left for a cycle that touches the instrument, which this one deliberately did not.
