# AMENDMENT — SP-EXT Q2: adjudicate the remaining 100, making it a census

Frozen and committed **before any of the 100 undrawn candidates was read.**

Parent: `PREREG_sp_external_corpus_2026_08_21.md` (`38b8428`),
`AMENDMENT_sp_ext_q2_sampling_2026_08_21.md` (`87b51fa`).

---

## what changes

The sampling amendment drew **40 of 140** because 420 agents would not complete in
one session. The 40 ran (`RESULT_sp_ext_q2_2026_08_21.md`): 3 accepted, 7.5%.

This extends adjudication to the **remaining 100**, under the identical unchanged
3-lens protocol. The result is a **census of the Q2 pool, not a sample of it.**

**This strictly removes a design weakness.** Sampling introduces variance and
invites the suspicion that a draw was lucky; a census cannot be sampled
favourably because nothing is left out. No inclusion rule, exclusion, lens, or
gate is altered.

## the reporting rule, fixed now

Two rates will exist and **both are published**:

- **the preregistered rate** — 3/40 = **7.5%**, from the sample drawn before
  inspection. This remains the primary figure, because it is the one whose
  procedure was frozen before its candidates were seen.
- **the census rate** — accepted / 140, over the full pool.

**I will not substitute whichever is more favourable.** If the census rate is
higher, the sample rate still leads; if it is lower, the census rate is reported
beside it with equal prominence. The gates in the parent prereg are applied to
**both**, and any disagreement between them is stated rather than resolved.

The already-adjudicated 40 are **not re-run**; their verdicts stand as recorded.

## gates

G1, G2, G3, G5 unchanged. In particular **G1's threshold of 12 is not rescaled**
now that the denominator grows, exactly as it was not rescaled when the
denominator shrank. A threshold that moves with the denominator is not a
threshold.

## what this still cannot fix

The gap named in `RESULT_sp_ext_q2_2026_08_21.md` stands, and a census of Q2 does
nothing about it:

> **Q2 requires a flattering constant to be REMOVED.** A fix that adds a guard
> *above an unchanged return* — the guard that was never written, finally written
> — matches neither query, and is invisible to this entire harvest.

That is a separate class needing a separate query and its own preregistration.
Enlarging Q2's denominator does not touch it, and no census result should be read
as though it did. **SP-EXT remains a lower bound and is never quoted as a rate.**
