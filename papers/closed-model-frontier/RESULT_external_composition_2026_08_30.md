a measurement — that roughly `260` of the `575` external verifications are sworn to non-claims.

## What follows

* On this laboratory's own documents, the volunteer channel is a large minority of the
  certificate. **On everyone else's, it is the certificate** — the majority of what `VERIFIED`
  means abroad is a value coincidence nothing asked the instrument to examine.
* Every certificate issued since `epistemics_summary` v1 exposes this split per document, so an
  external adopter's report now shows their own `414/575` equivalent without any of this
  analysis. `styxx-oathready` reads the annotation directly (it previously re-derived

  verified count is not an internal nicety. Abroad it is the difference between a certificate
