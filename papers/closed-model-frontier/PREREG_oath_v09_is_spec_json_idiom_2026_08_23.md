# PREREG — OATH v0.9: `is_spec` recall, and the price of buying it with silence

Fathom Lab · 2026-08-23 · frozen BEFORE any change to `styxx/certify.py`. Bars below never move;
a missed bar ⇒ revert that clause and publish the negative, nothing ships. No optional stopping;
no bar moves after this commit.

Provenance: `PREREG_oath_v07_precision_obligation_2026_08_22.md` named this class, sized it, and
put it out of scope in one paragraph — "`is_spec` requires an operator character in the
18-character window immediately before the token. A bar written in JSON syntax puts its operator
in a separate `"op"` field, and 157 of 157 such bar tokens repo-wide are rescued zero times today
… `is_spec` JSON-idiom recall is owed and out of scope." Its outcome table hands the lead here
explicitly. First attempt in this family; it resurrects no buried claim.

## The defect (measured, pre-fix)

`certify_doc` builds `pre` as the 18 characters before the token and `post` as the rest of the
line, then declares a SPECIFICATION when `pre` ends in `≥ ≤ < > =` or carries bar vocabulary, or
when `post` opens with `CI`/`confidence` or with `bar`/`threshold`/`gate`. A specification abstains,
because its receipt is the preregistration and not a result JSON.

Both halves of the miss are measured in `oath_v09_isspec_census.json`, generated by
`oath_v09_isspec_census.py` at the shipped verifier before any edit.

**Half one — the JSON idiom.** Across the 1,068 markdown documents under `papers/`, **145 tokens**
in **42 documents** sit in JSON value position inside an object that carries a comparison-operator
field: `{"metric": "voice_preference_rate", "op": ">=", "value": 0.75}`. **0 of 145** are rescued
by the shipped `is_spec`. (The v0.7 note said 157 of 157; this cycle's detector, committed and
re-runnable, reads 145 of 145. The discrepancy is a detector definition, not a disagreement — the
recall is zero either way.)

The class is not hypothetical. `PREDICTION_h1_human_islands_2026_08_06.certificate.json`, a
committed certificate, currently attests four of them, and two of the four are the failure in its
purest form:

| line | claim | certificate says |
|---|---|---|
| L56 | `"H1_islands": {"metric": "dip_test_p_subject_accuracy", "op": "<=", "value": 0.05}` | VERIFIED ← `b45_result.json:null_expectation_k20` |
| L57 | `"H2_cliff": {"metric": "sigmoid_minus_linear_r2", "op": ">=", "value": 0.05}` | VERIFIED ← `b45_result.json:null_expectation_k20` |

Two different preregistered bars — a dip-test *p*-value and an R² — are sworn to against **one
unrelated leaf**, whose only qualification is holding the float `0.05`. The oath is being taken on
a coincidence.

**Half two — the bar noun.** Prose that names the bar AFTER the number ("clears the 0.10 floor",
"against the 25 floor", "below the arc's 0.15 floor") also escapes, because the noun is in `post`
and the shipped `post` rule knows only `bar`/`threshold`/`gate`. In the 137 certified documents
whose receipts all resolve, **38 tokens** are missed this way; 37 of them are VERIFIED today.

**The two halves are not the same defect, and the census says why.** Of the 145 JSON-idiom tokens,
**0 sit on lines the shipped OBLIGATION predicate binds.** Of the 38 bar-noun tokens, **36 do.**
Only an obligated token can be CAUGHT when it is doctored, so only an obligated token has a catch
that an abstention rule can destroy. Mutating one significant digit in each of the 38 bar-noun
tokens at the shipped verifier, over ten seeds:

| | mean | range |
|---|---|---|
| mutants CAUGHT (UNGROUNDED) — a catch an abstention rule would destroy | 18.7 | 16–22 |
| mutants FALSELY ATTESTED (VERIFIED against an unrelated leaf) — removed by abstention | 17.4 | 14–20 |

The two columns overlap and the per-seed net changes sign, so **no single seed settles which way
the trade runs, and reporting one would be cherry-picking.** What does not move with the seed is
the column that matters: an `is_spec` extension takes the CATCH column to **zero at every seed**,
because the predicate reads the characters around the token and a one-digit substitution leaves
them unchanged. On this half, abstention does not detect the tamper; it stops looking.

This is the standing hazard in this program written out as arithmetic: `is_spec` moves claims to
ABSTAIN, and a clause that abstains generously improves every tamper metric while destroying
coverage. The cycle is therefore scoped to the half where the trade is measurably free, and the
half where it is not is preregistered as a control and expected to fail.

## The change (exact, frozen)

Two severable clauses, appended to the `is_spec` disjunction at its existing site in
`certify_doc`, changing nothing else. `is_spec` yields only ABSTAIN, so both are DEMOTE-ONLY.

```python
V09_IS_SPEC_JSON_IDIOM = True    # primary
V09_IS_SPEC_BAR_NOUN   = False   # control, retained OFF with its measurement

_JSON_OP_FIELD = re.compile(r'"(?:op|operator|cmp|comparison|direction|sense)"\s*:\s*"\s*'
                            r'(?:<=|>=|!=|==|<|>|=)\s*"')
_JSON_BAR_KEY  = re.compile(r'"(?:value|bar|threshold|floor|ceiling|cutoff|min|max|alpha|target'
                            r'|bound)"\s*:\s*$')
_BAR_NOUN_POST = re.compile(r"^[ \t\-]{0,2}(?:floors?|ceilings?|cutoffs?|caps?|bounds?)\b", re.I)

# ... immediately after the existing is_spec assignment:
if V09_IS_SPEC_JSON_IDIOM and not is_spec:
    is_spec = bool(_JSON_BAR_KEY.search(pre)) and bool(_JSON_OP_FIELD.search(ctx))
if V09_IS_SPEC_BAR_NOUN and not is_spec:
    is_spec = bool(_BAR_NOUN_POST.match(post))
```

**Why the operator field must be required.** `"value": 0.75` alone is a JSON key/value pair and
says nothing about specification. The comparison-operator field on the same object is what makes
the number a *bound on some other quantity* rather than a recorded one. Requiring both is what
keeps the clause off result receipts transcribed into documents.

**Why the bar-noun slack is two characters.** Swept pre-fix at 0/2/4/8/12 characters of filler
between the token and the noun. At 0 the clause never fires. At 2 it fires on 38 tokens and every
one reads as a bar. At 4 and above it starts taking measurements that merely *precede* a
parenthesised bar — `0.5362318840579711 (floor 0.50)`, `0.9853 (rand floor 0.5124)` — and at 8 it
takes `0.954` out of "(clean read 0.954) floors at ~0.62", where `floors` is a verb. The slack is
frozen at 2 so that the control tests the doctrine and not a sloppy regex.

**Everything else UNTOUCHED.** `_TRIGGERS`, `_TRIGGERS_CORR`, `_NUM`, `_SHAISH`, `_DATEISH`,
`_VERSIONISH`, `_MD_STRUCTURE`, `extract_numbers`, `receipt_values`, `_BULK_PATHS`, `_match`,
`_ulp_neighbour` and every v0.5 / v0.6.2 / v0.7 class are byte-identical. The status ladder order
is byte-identical. The clauses sit inside `is_spec`, which is already first in the ladder, so they
move no token that `is_hist`, `is_notation`, `derived_ref` or `hits` currently decides — they only
enlarge the set that reaches ABSTAIN before any of those are consulted.

## The design that is rejected here, and why (receipt-side binding)

The obvious alternative reads the receipt, not the document: a claim whose only matching leaf sits
under a `frozen_gates` / `kill_gates` container is grounding in a SPEC leaf, so abstain it. The
census sizes this at **63 VERIFIED tokens** in the certified corpus, 28 of which the doc-side
bar-noun predicate already reaches.

It is rejected in advance, unbuilt, for a structural reason: **it consults `hits`.** Doctor the
number and the match to the `frozen_gates` leaf disappears, the clause stops firing, and the mutant
proceeds down the ladder to whatever unrelated leaf holds its new value — so the clause is absent
in exactly the case it exists to handle, and its clean-corpus behaviour is not evidence about its
behaviour under tamper. This is v0.7's lesson restated one register over: an obligation that
consults `hits` cannot gate, and a *declassification* that consults `hits` cannot survive its own
threat model. Sized, named, not built.

## Frozen adjudication definition (to remove post-hoc judgment)

A token is a **BAR** iff it is a value the document fixes IN ADVANCE as a condition on some other
quantity — a gate threshold, floor, ceiling, cutoff, cap, tolerance, α, or required minimum — such
that its authority is a preregistration and no experimental outcome could change it without a new
one. A token is a **MEASUREMENT** iff it is produced by running something: an observed rate, count,
statistic, or any value the document reports as an outcome. A number that is a bar in one document
and an outcome in another is adjudicated by **the role it plays on its own line**.

**Anything not clearly a BAR is a MEASUREMENT. Ties resolve MEASUREMENT — against the clause.**

The adjudication sample is drawn and frozen inside `oath_v09_isspec_census.json`
(`frozen_adjudication_sample`, seed 9, n=25) from the ADJUDICATION FRAME: every `extract_numbers`
token on a `papers/**/*.md` line carrying a comparison-operator field, in value position or not.
The frame is defined by the operator field alone, so the clause's own value-position requirement
does not define its own test set. **Disclosed limitation: the frame is nearly coextensive with the
clause (146 tokens, of which 145 are in value position), so it bounds precision tightly and
supplies little independent evidence about recall.** The load-bearing evidence in this cycle is the
safety measurement (G3/G4), not the recall measurement.

## Battery + arms (harness `run_oath_v09_battery.py`, seeds frozen below)

Sampling frame for every corpus leg: documents under `papers/**` carrying a `*.certificate.json`,
excluding `anc/` staging copies, whose recorded receipts ALL resolve next to the document with

## Frozen gates

- **G1 — RECALL (gated, two-armed).** On the frozen 146-token adjudication frame,
  `V09_IS_SPEC_JSON_IDIOM` ON rescues **≥ 140**; the shipped verifier OFF rescues **≤ 2**.
  **If the ON arm does not strictly exceed the OFF arm the run is VOID**, whatever the other gates
  read: a battery reporting the same number in both arms is not measuring the clause.
- **G2 — ADJUDICATED PRECISION (gated).** Of the 25 tokens in `frozen_adjudication_sample` that
  the clause abstains, **≥ 24 must hand-adjudicate BAR** under the definition above. Ties resolve
  against the clause. One token that adjudicates MEASUREMENT and is abstained anyway is a false
  abstention on an honest document.
- **G3 — COVERAGE BOUND (gated).** Across all 137 resolvable documents,
  `V09_IS_SPEC_JSON_IDIOM` converts **≤ 2** baseline-VERIFIED tokens to ABSTAIN. This is the gate
  that refuses the metric that rewards silence: a clause paying for its tamper number with honest
  coverage fails here regardless of how good its tamper number looks.
- **G4 — NO SILENCED ACCUSATION (gated).** With the primary clause ON: **0** baseline-UNGROUNDED
  tokens converted to ABSTAIN, and **0** certificates flipping OATH-FAILED → OATH-HELD. Both bars
  are zero. An abstention rule that quiets an existing accusation is doing the exact harm the
  instrument exists to prevent.
- **G5 — SEVERABILITY (gated).** With `V09_IS_SPEC_JSON_IDIOM` and `V09_IS_SPEC_BAR_NOUN` both
  OFF, the ledger is status-identical to `oath_v09_baseline_ledger.json` across all 137 documents.
  Any difference → the clauses are not severable and do not ship.
- **G6 — CATCH PRESERVATION / POSITIVE CONTROL (gated, two-armed; decides the second clause).**
  On the frozen 38-token bar-noun roster, one significant digit perturbed per token at seeds
  1–10, the ON arm's caught-UNGROUNDED count must be **≥ the OFF arm's at every seed**.
  Pre-fix census: OFF ∈ [16, 22]; the clause is expected to read 0 and **this gate is expected to
  FAIL.** It is preregistered anyway for two reasons. It is the disciplined test of the second
  clause, which is otherwise an untested opinion. And it is the **positive control for the safety
  instrument**: G3 and G4 assert that the primary clause destroys nothing, and a screen with
  unknown recall reporting zero on a corpus is indistinguishable from a screen that cannot see.
  G6 is what proves this battery can see catch destruction when catch destruction is present.
- **G7 — SUITE (gated).** `python -m pytest tests -q` green; `ruff` clean (a red lint gates the
  test step in this repository's CI and has silently masked the whole suite before); `py_compile`
  on every touched `.py`.

## Asserted invariants — NOT gated

A leg that cannot fail must not gate. Both of these are properties of the construction, asserted
in the test suite and reported in the RESULT, and neither is counted as a passed bar.

- **I1 — mutation-stability.** An `is_spec` clause reads only the characters surrounding the
  token, and a single-digit substitution preserves the token's length and therefore both windows.
  A token the clause abstains stays abstained under mutation. Consequently the ON arm's
  false-attestation count on any class the clause reaches is **0 by construction, not by
  measurement**, and reporting it as a catch would launder an identity as a result.
- **I2 — demote-only.** `is_spec` yields only ABSTAIN, so neither clause can create an UNGROUNDED
  token and no certificate can flip OATH-HELD → OATH-FAILED.

## Outcome table (pre-committed)

- G1–G5 and G7 all pass → **`V09_JSON_IDIOM_SHIPS`.** `V09_IS_SPEC_JSON_IDIOM` ships True. The
  RESULT publishes the corpus delta, the adjudication verdicts, both arms of every gate, and the
  residual: the number of JSON-idiom tokens that sit in NO fully-resolvable certified document and
  therefore gain nothing measurable today.
- G1 misses → `V09_INSUFFICIENT_RECALL`; revert the clause, publish the negative.
- G1's ON arm fails to exceed its OFF arm → `V09_BATTERY_VOID`; no verdict is recorded, the
  harness is the defect, and the family waits for a corrected harness.
- G2 misses → `V09_FALSE_ABSTENTION`; revert, publish. Raising catches while adding a single false
  accusation does not ship, and the mirror rule holds: raising recall while abstaining one honest
  measurement does not ship either.
- G3 misses → `V09_COVERAGE_TRADE`; revert, publish.
- G4 misses → `V09_SILENCED_ACCUSATION`; revert, publish.
- G5 fails → `V09_NOT_SEVERABLE`; revert, publish.
- G6 fails → `V09_BARNOUN_CATCH_REGRESSION`. `V09_IS_SPEC_BAR_NOUN` is retained at **False** with
  its measurement recorded in the source comment, exactly as `V05_APPROX_NOTATION = False` is
  retained with its own, so that flipping one flag reproduces the negative. **This outcome does
  not block the primary clause**, which is severable and judged on G1–G5/G7 alone.
- G7 fails → `V09_SUITE_RED`; fix the suite or revert. No verifier ships with a red suite.
- No second attempt inside this cycle. A miss on the primary clause hands the lead to status-level
  claim→field binding for floats, which is the instrument that actually attacks false attestation.
