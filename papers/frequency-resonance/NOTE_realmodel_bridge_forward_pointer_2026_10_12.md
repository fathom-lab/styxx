The July RESULT is certified. `CERT_realmodel_bridge.json` binds it by its `document_sha256`,
`6d9bf383e50360c36cae0e277e35b8a0e2b6e67f94797addd7c340ffdea8cda7`, and the attempt-three RESULT swears both that
field and that the July document hashes to it (a quote span and a hash span, sworn at commit `d0411b2d`). An edit in
place would leave the certificate naming bytes the file no longer holds, so the pointer could land inside the
document only under a fresh certificate. The attempt-three RESULT, in its second defect in the record, names the two
forms the pointer may take — an edit with a fresh certificate, or a note beside the document — and leaves the choice
to the operator.
