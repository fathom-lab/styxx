# ORG — the chart that cannot lie about its own boxes

Fathom Lab · 2026-08-28 · Receipt: `styxx_org_census.json`. Generated by `papers/styxx_org_census.py`.

## The genre, and what is wrong with it

Personal-AI-org diagrams are having a moment. The form is always the same: a human operator at the
top, an orchestration layer beneath, and a fan of specialist boxes — an intel gatherer, a content
crafter, a trader — each carrying a small status lamp reading `ONLINE` or `BOOTING`.

The lamps are typed. Nothing checks them. `BOOTING` is the polite label for a box that does not
exist, and a viewer has no way to tell which of the two states in front of them is a measurement
and which is an intention.

**A status that asserts its own health is the defect this laboratory exists to document.** It is
SP-1 in `benchmarks/silent_pass/CORPUS.md`, and we found it in our own suite eight days into
looking for it elsewhere: the silent-pass benchmark was skipping in CI on every shallow checkout,
and the suite went green, because a skip is green.

So this is the same picture with the lamps wired to something.

## What is computed rather than typed

For each of the 11 roles:

* **implemented** — the module is on disk, with its line count read from it;
* **tests** — its test files are *run*, during the census, and the pass and fail counts recorded;
* **receipts** — how many committed JSON receipts carry its name;
* **disclosed defect** — what is currently wrong with it, **with the citation checked**. A defect
  claim whose document has vanished reads as disclosure while disclosing nothing, so a missing
  document downgrades the role to `CITATION_MISSING` rather than passing quietly.

231 tests are executed by the census itself, and 0 of them fail. No box can report `ONLINE`
because someone typed `ONLINE`.

## The positive control, which is the whole argument

A status column that cannot report a bad value is not reporting a good one either. So the chart
carries a **declared positive control**, and it is deliberately the box the source diagram drew:
`TRADER`, captioned *paper trading → live*, lamp set to `BOOTING`.

Ours points at a module that is not on disk and comes back `ABSENT`. The census refuses to emit a
chart if it reports anything else.

That column has now failed three times. Twice on purpose — the control, and its assertion. Once
by accident, on the census's first run, against its own author: a defect citation pointed at
`papers/REPLICATIONS.md`, and that file lives at the repository root. The role went
`CITATION_MISSING` until it was fixed. **The mechanism caught its author before it caught
anything else**, which is the only evidence worth having that it works.

## Nine of eleven roles disclose a defect

Not as a confession, as a design rule: a chart on which every lamp is green is the same genre of
lie as a lamp nobody checks.

The verifier at the centre of this repository declines to check roughly two in five of the
checkable claims in our own corpus, and about one in five of the verifications it does make are
sworn to things that are not claims. That is written on its box. The null-rule check cannot see
overfitting, and that is written on its box. The blind panel is three seats of one model family
whose 98% unanimity is a correlated-error ceiling rather than agreement, and that is written on
its box.

Two roles disclose nothing. They are marked `ONLINE_NO_DEFECT_DISCLOSED` — not `HEALTHY` — because
"we have not found a defect" and "there is no defect" are different sentences and only one of them
is supported.

## The recursion, stated because it is load-bearing

This document is OATH-certified against the census receipt. The instrument that certified it is
the `VERIFIER` box in the chart it certifies. If that box is broken, this document's certificate
is worth less than it looks — and the box says what is broken about it.

That is not a paradox and it is not decoration. It is the only honest way to publish an org chart
from a laboratory whose subject is unverified claims: the chart is a document full of claims, so
it carries its receipt like every other document here, and it inherits every limitation of the
thing that checked it.
