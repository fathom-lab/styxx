darkflobi · ledger

darkflobi is the lab's agent (fathom-lab/styxx). since 2026-10-24 a gate at its telegram exits writes one hash-chained line per outbound attempt: hashes, verdicts and counts, never message text or chat ids. every night at 00:00 new york time the day's head is posted in public at t.me/DFledger. this page carries the ledger's bytes and re-derives the chain in your browser: every number below is computed here, from the embedded bytes, when you open it.

the days

public sends by the gate's decision in shadow: clean (every numeral bound, or none present), marked (a numeral with no receipt), would refuse (a lint hit, or what the frozen rule refuses). operator dm lines are counted apart: they are hashes like the rest and outside the measured population.

break it

change one line in a copy and re-derive the copy. a flipped field breaks the next line's link. a forger who then rewrites every later digest gets a copy that re-derives, with a different head, and the heads posted in public no longer match it: that is what the nightly post is for.

what this is not


    

  • not a transcript: no message text, no chat id, no session key is in these lines; destinations are salted hashes.
    • 

  • not a truth check: the gate counts numerals and binds the ones that have a committed receipt, and it reads a word list; it does not read whether a sentence is true.
    • 

  • not a proof of completeness: the chain shows that no line was changed, dropped or reordered without the digests disagreeing; it cannot show that every send got a line. the census at the window's close reads the chats for that.
    • 

  • not whole where a line is withheld: a withheld line's digest is published and its fields are not, so you bridge it on the lab's word; the lines between the previous posted head and that line are then not pinned for you. the status line above counts them.
    • 

  • not enforcement yet: the gate runs in shadow (required_present: false on every line). "would refuse" is what it would have blocked; it blocked nothing.
    • 

  • not signed: anyone holding the file can recompute the chain. what fixes the bytes and their order is the daily post at t.me/DFledger, outside the box; the clock in the lines is the gate's own statement.
    • 

  • not every exit: the gate sits on the telegram exits; what leaves by another route is not on it.
