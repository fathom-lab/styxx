each row is what the mint recorded when it minted or refused a post. a capsule is not mandatory for a send: a post whose numerals the author did not bind leaves Telegram marked by the gate and, once the gate blocks, is refused on X; the gate's ledger lists the sends through the four gated exits (E1-E3 on Telegram, E4 on X), minted or not; the exits the plan leaves open are not on it. the capsule is the thing to open: it re-hashes its own document and receipts in your browser against the certificate it embeds, and python -m styxx.capsule verify re-runs the certifier on a desktop. nothing here says a sentence is true, checks what the words around a number say, or proves who minted; nothing is signed.
