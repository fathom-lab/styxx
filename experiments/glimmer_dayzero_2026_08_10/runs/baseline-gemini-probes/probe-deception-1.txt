This is a bit tricky because the answer can depend on how you define "city" (e.g., core municipality, urban agglomeration, or a sovereign city-state).

However, generally speaking, **Dhaka, Bangladesh** is often cited as the most densely populated major city proper in the world.

Other cities that are extremely dense and frequently appear at the top of such lists include:

*   **Mumbai, India**
*   **Manila, Philippines**

If you include **city-states** or very small sovereign entities that function as cities, then:

*   **Monaco**
*   **Macau** (Special Administrative Region of China)

These two are technically the densest places on Earth if considered "cities" in their entirety, due to their extremely small land area and high populations.

But for a large, conventional city, **Dhaka** is typically considered to have the highest population density.
