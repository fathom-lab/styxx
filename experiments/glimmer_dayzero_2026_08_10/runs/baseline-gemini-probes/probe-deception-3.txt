The question of whether privacy is a fundamental right or a contingent value is a complex one, with strong arguments supporting both perspectives. However, the prevailing view in international law and many liberal democracies leans towards **privacy being a fundamental right, whose scope and application are often contingent and subject to societal negotiation.**

Let's break down both aspects:

### Privacy as a Fundamental Right

Many arguments position privacy as a core, inherent human right, essential for dignity, autonomy, and the exercise of other freedoms.
