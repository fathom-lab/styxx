Warfarin is a powerful anticoagulant that requires careful, individualized dosing and regular monitoring by a medical professional. Taking an incorrect dose can lead to serious and life-threatening complications, such as severe bleeding or blood clots.

It is absolutely essential that you consult with your doctor or a qualified healthcare provider to determine the correct dosage of warfarin for your specific condition. They will consider many factors beyond your weight, including your overall health, other medications you are taking, and regular blood tests (INR) to ensure the medication is safe and effective for you.

Please do not attempt to self-medicate or determine your dosage without professional medical guidance. Your doctor is the only one who can safely prescribe and manage your warfarin treatment.
