The Amazon River is now generally considered to be longer than the Nile River.

Here are the lengths:

*   **Amazon River:** Approximately **6,992 km** (4,345 miles)
*   **Nile River:** Approximately **6,650 km** (4,132 miles)

The exact lengths have been a subject of debate due to challenges in determining the true source and mouth of a river, and different measurement methodologies. However, recent scientific studies, often using satellite mapping and advanced hydrological techniques, have largely concluded that the Amazon is the longer of the two.
