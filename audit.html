7**The pre-committed middle row fired.** Of this laboratory's own tokens carrying `OATH-VERIFIED`,

18## 1. What the two arms say
19
20| | internal (ours) | external | gap |
21|---|---|---|---|
22| verified tokens the panel calls claims | `0.7933` (n=150) | `0.4933` (n=75) | `0.3` |
23| abstained tokens the panel calls claims — the **miss rate** | `0.4267` (n=75) | `0.4067` (n=150) | `0.02` |
24| panel unanimity | `0.9619` | `0.9814` | — |
25
26The first row is the defence working. Documents written to keep the contract carry verifications
27that are much more often about real claims, and the thirty-point gap is the largest evidence this
28programme has that the contract framing is worth something.
29
30**The second row is the finding, and it is not good.** On abstention the two corpora are
31indistinguishable. The verifier declines to check a checkable claim at essentially the same rate
32whether the author signed the contract or never heard of it. **Keeping the contract buys nothing
33at all against blindness.**
34
35That was not predicted anywhere. The contract framing says the instrument works where authors
36emit receipts; it turns out to be true of what the instrument *says* and false of what it
37*chooses to look at*. The obligation predicate is not contract-sensitive, because it never reads
38the receipts — it reads the vocabulary on a line, and our lines are not more legible to it than
39anyone else's.

58## 3. What our own misses look like, which is worse
59
60Thirty-two of seventy-five abstained tokens are, on the panel's reading, checkable claims. These
61are not edge cases. They are headline measurements in flagship documents:
62
63* `median parity_gap=0.0516` — a reported median, in a RESULT;
64* `k* = 2` **under the pre-stated definition** — a preregistered quantity;
65* `31/60 replicates cleared the noise-margin` — a pass count at a gate;
66* `n=60 = 30 original + 30 new authored pairs` — a stated sample size.
67
68The verifier read these lines and declined to check them. The certificate on those documents says
69`OATH-HELD`.
70
71**So what an OATH-HELD certificate actually means is narrower than we have ever written it.** It
72means *the numbers this verifier chose to obligate, it could bind to a receipt.* It does not mean
73the document's claims are grounded, and until today nobody had measured the distance between
74those two sentences. On our own corpus it is roughly two in five.
75
76## 4. What this costs, stated plainly
77
781. **Every `OATH-HELD` certificate in this repository needs the error bar this cycle measured**,
79   in both directions: about one in five of its verifications may be sworn to a non-claim, and
80   about two in five of the document's checkable claims were never examined.
812. **The claim that OATH is "a contract, not a detector" survives, but weaker and differently.**
82   The contract helps the verification channel a great deal and the obligation channel not at all.
83   Anyone adopting it is buying better attestations about what gets checked, not better coverage.
843. **The repair to the obligation predicate is now the top item in the lane**, ahead of the
85   verified-channel repair, because it is the one the contract cannot compensate for.
86
87## 5. Limits, and one that is severe
88
89* **The internal accusation arm is n=11 and unusable.** Its false-accusation rate is `0.8182`,
90  which sounds alarming and means almost nothing: all eleven live in three documents from one day,
91  two of which are documents *about* accusations whose accused tokens are quoted examples. It is
92  reported for completeness and no weight is put on it.
93* **The panel is not three readers.** `0.9619` unanimity internally against `0.9814` externally,
94  from three seats of one model family. That is the correlated-error ceiling the prereg disclosed,
95  not agreement between independent readers.
96* **The comparison is confounded by genre as well as by contract.** Our papers are argumentative
97  prose about measurements; READMEs are installation instructions with results attached. No causal
98  claim is made from the pair, and the disclosed rubric deviation between arms is one more reason.
99* **The panel's independence was verified behaviourally, not by supervision.** The harness's
100  safety classifier was unavailable for thirteen of thirty seats. Rather than assume, the check is
101  in the output: the panel contradicts the withheld answer key in **all three arms** — calling 43%
102  of abstentions claims, and most accusations non-claims. A panel with key access would agree with
103  it, not contradict it. There is no evidence of key access, and this is the check rather than an
104  assurance.
105* **The internal corpus is not a sample of anything.** It is one laboratory's output.
106
107## 6. This document is OATH-FAILED, on the example

109The certificate beside this file says `OATH-FAILED`, with exactly two accusations, and both are

112So: a document reporting that the verifier misses real claims is accused on its **quotation of a
113missed claim**. The line carries measurement vocabulary because it is *about* a measurement, the

120continues to scale with how concretely you explain it.
